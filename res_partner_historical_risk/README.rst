===========================
Res Partner Historical Risk
===========================

This module adds historical tracking for partner credit risk in Odoo.

It stores periodic snapshots of partner risk values, allowing traceability and analysis of risk evolution over time.

The historical data is automatically generated by a scheduled action and can also be created manually through a server action in the partner views.

Features
--------

- New model `res.partner.historical.risk` to store risk snapshots per partner.
- Automatic creation of historical records via scheduled action (cron).
- Manual generation of historical risk records from the partner list view.
- Multi-company support with company-based record rules.
- Read-only historical records (no manual edition allowed).
- Dedicated tree and search views for analysis.
- Grouping by Partner and Date in search view.
- Pivot support for risk analysis.
- Menu entry under Accounting Receivables for easy access.

Data Model
----------

**res.partner.historical.risk**

- Partner (partner_id): Related contact.
- Company (company_id): Related company (inherited from partner).
- Date (date): Snapshot date.
- Risk Total Amount (risk_total_amount): Total risk amount.
- Credit Policy Amount (credit_policy_amount): Credit policy amount.

Security
--------

- Access rights restricted to Accounting Users.
- Multi-company record rule applied:
  - Users can only see records belonging to their companies.
  - Records without company are also allowed.

Automation
----------

- Cron job (`cron_create_historical_risk`) runs weekly.
- Generates historical snapshots for all active partners.

- Server action available in partner views:
  - Allows manual creation of historical risk for selected partners.
