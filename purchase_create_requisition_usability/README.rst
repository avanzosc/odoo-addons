=====================================
Purchase Create Requisition Usability
=====================================

This module improves usability when working with purchase agreements.

- When creating a purchase agreement from a purchase order, it copies the
	order lines into the new agreement.
- It enables creating agreements directly from the RFQ agreement field.
- It broadens the agreement domain in RFQ to show agreements not in
	``cancel`` or ``done`` state, while keeping vendor and company filtering.
