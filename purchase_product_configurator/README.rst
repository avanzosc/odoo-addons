=============================
Purchase Product Configurator
=============================

This module adds the standard Odoo product configurator to purchase
order lines.

On a draft purchase order, the pencil button next to a configurable
product opens the same configurator used on sales order lines.
Confirming the configuration updates the selected product variant,
attribute values, and quantity on the purchase order line.
