=================================
Stock Move In/Out Quantity Fields
=================================

This module adds three computed fields to stock operations for better inventory tracking:

* **Incoming Quantity**: Positive when stock moves from a non-internal location to an internal location.
* **Outgoing Quantity**: Negative when stock moves from an internal location to a non-internal location.
* **Difference**: Sum of Incoming and Outgoing quantities (positive for net inflows, negative for net outflows).
