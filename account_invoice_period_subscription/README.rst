===================================
Account invoice period subscription
===================================

* In account invoice new fields: "Start date period" and "End date period".
* When creating the invoice from a subscription, set the period start date to
  the earliest "Start Date" among the generated invoice lines, and the period
  end date to the latest "End Date" among those lines.
