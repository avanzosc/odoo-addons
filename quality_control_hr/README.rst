==================
Quality Control Hr
==================

This module extends the functionality of quality_control_oca by adding employee-related information to quality inspections and simplifying the management of measurement data.

Features
--------

**Quality Inspection Management**

- Adds the Quality Manager field to quality inspections, allowing users to assign an employee responsible for quality control.

- Adds the Person who performed the measurement field to the inspection header.

- Adds the Measurement Date field to the inspection header.

- Automatically propagates the measurement employee and date from the inspection header to inspection lines that do not already have these values.

**Quality Inspection Lines**

- Adds the Person who performed the measurement and Measurement Date fields to quality inspection lines.

- Automatically assigns the measurement employee and date from the inspection header when inspection lines are generated from a quality test.

- Automatically fills in missing measurement information on existing inspection lines when the corresponding header fields are updated.

- Automatically assigns the inspection header values when new inspection lines are created, provided that no specific values have been supplied.

- Allows users to manually modify the measurement employee and date for individual inspection lines without affecting the other lines.

- Preserves existing measurement information on inspection lines when updating the inspection header.

- Adds the possibility to search and group inspection lines by measurement employee and measurement date.

**Automatic Measurement Data Assignment**

The module supports different workflows for entering measurement information:

- Header before test assignment: When the measurement employee and date are specified before assigning a quality test, the generated inspection lines inherit these values.

- Test before header assignment: When the quality test has already generated inspection lines, entering the measurement employee and date in the inspection header automatically fills in missing values on those lines.

- Inspection duplication: When an inspection is duplicated and its lines are regenerated by a compatible module, the new lines inherit the measurement information from the duplicated inspection header.

- Individual adjustments: Users can override the measurement employee or date on specific inspection lines while keeping the values assigned to the remaining lines.

Usage
-----

#. Navigate to Quality Control > Inspections.
#. Open or create a quality inspection.
#. Set the Quality Manager, if applicable.
#. Specify the Person who performed the measurement and Measurement Date in the inspection header.
#. Assign a quality test or update an existing inspection.
#. The measurement information will be automatically assigned to inspection lines where the corresponding fields are empty.
#. If a specific line requires a different employee or measurement date, modify its values directly.
