=====================================
Account Payment Partner Remove Margin
=====================================

Overview
========

The **Account Payment Partner Remove Margin** module removes the margin style from the payment mode section of the invoice report. This module targets the `account_payment_partner.report_invoice_payment_mode` template and applies a style change to remove any margin applied to the payment mode note.

Features
========

- **Remove Margin from Payment Mode**:
  - Removes the margin applied to the `payment_mode_id.note` field in the invoice payment report.

Usage
=====

1. **Install the Module**:
   
   - Install the **Account Payment Partner Remove Margin** module from the Apps menu.

2. **View the Changes**:
 
   - After installation, the payment mode section in the invoice report will no longer have a margin.
