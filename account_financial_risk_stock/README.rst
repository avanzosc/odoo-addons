============================
Account financial risk stock
============================
* In partner new field "Block picking in case of exceeded risk".
* When the picking is validated, if the previous field is activated,
  the customer's "risk exception" field is also activated, the error will be
  displayed: "This customer has blocked the issuance of picking due to risk
  limits."
