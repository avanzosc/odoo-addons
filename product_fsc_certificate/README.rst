This module allows you to identify products that have FSC (Forest Stewardship Council) certification.

The module adds the following functionality:

* Adds a boolean field "Is FSC certificate" in product template to mark products with FSC certification.

* Automatically propagates the FSC status to commercial documents:

  * **Sale Orders**: Computes whether any line contains an FSC-certified product.
  * **Delivery Orders**: Computes whether any move contains an FSC-certified product.
  * **Invoices**: Computes whether any invoice line contains an FSC-certified product.

* Modifies reports to display the FSC certification:

  * Adds an asterisk (*) next to FSC-certified product names in line items.
  * Displays the FSC certificate logo and number at the bottom of documents.
  * Applies to: Sale Order reports, Delivery Slip, and Invoice reports.

* Adds a server action "FSC Update Orders" to recalculate FSC status for selected products.

The FSC certification mark and number are configured at the company level, allowing each company to display its own certification details.

Configuration
=============

To configure the FSC certificate for your company:

1. Go to Settings > Companies > Companies.

2. Open your company form.

3. Go to the "FSC Certificate" tab.

4. Fill in the following fields:

   * **FSC Certificate Logo**: Upload the image/logo of your FSC certification.
   * **FSC Certificate Number**: Enter your FSC certificate number (e.g., BMC-COC-010044).

5. Save the company.

The certificate logo and number will be displayed on reports (Sale Orders, Delivery Orders, Invoices) when the document contains FSC-certified products.

Usage
=====

To use this module, you need to:

1. Configure the FSC certificate at the company level (see Configuration section).

2. Mark products as FSC-certified:

   * Go to Sales > Products > Products.
   * Open a product form.
   * In the "Options" tab, check the "Is FSC certificate" field.
   * Save the product.

3. The FSC status will automatically propagate to:

   * **Sale Orders**: When a sale order contains an FSC-certified product, the order is marked as FSC.
   * **Delivery Orders**: When a delivery contains FSC-certified products, the picking is marked as FSC.
   * **Invoices**: When an invoice contains FSC-certified products, the invoice is marked as FSC.

4. Reports will automatically display:

   * An asterisk (*) next to FSC-certified product names.
   * The FSC certificate logo and number at the bottom of the document.

5. To update FSC status for existing orders:

   * Go to Sales > Products > Products.
   * Select the FSC-certified products.
   * Click on Action > FSC Update Orders.
