====================================
Purchase Supplierinfo Delivery Delay
====================================

* Adds a default delivery lead time field on vendors.
* When creating a vendor pricelist line, the vendor delivery lead time is
  copied to the line delivery lead time.
* Adds an action to assign the vendor delivery lead time to the selected vendor
  pricelist lines.
* Shows the vendor default delivery lead time on vendor pricelist lines.
* Adds a filter to find vendor pricelist lines where the vendor delivery lead
  time is different from the line delivery lead time.
