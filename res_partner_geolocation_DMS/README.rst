===========================
Res Partner Geolocation DMS
===========================

* Adds computed fields "Latitude (DMS)" and "Longitude (DMS)" to partners.
* Converts `partner_latitude` and `partner_longitude` to Degrees, Minutes, Seconds format.
* Fields appear after "Geo Location Date" in the Partner Assignment tab.
