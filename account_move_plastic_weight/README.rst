===========================
Account Move Plastic Weight
===========================

Calculates and stores on the invoice the total weight (in kg) of recycled plastic and non‑recycled plastic (subject to IPNR) for all included products.

Features:
- Adds two fields to the invoice: total non‑recycled plastic weight and recycled plastic weight.
- Manual server action to recalculate the weights (visible in the invoice action menu).
- Automatic recalculation when confirming the invoice.
