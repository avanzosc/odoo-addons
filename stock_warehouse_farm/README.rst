====================
Warehouse Farm Data
====================

* Adds farm-related fields to "Warehouse" (area, capacity, ownership, REGA
  numbers, distances, activity, farm type, farmer and tax entity).
* Adds "Section" (category type) to "Warehouse", "Stock Location",
  "Product Category" and "Product".
* Adds "Product Family" classification.
* Adds "Picking Batch Stage" configuration and a stage on
  "Stock Picking Batch".
* Adds origin/destination Section to "Operation Type", "Transfer",
  "Stock Move" and "Stock Move Line".
* Adds product category and section information to "Stock Quant".
* Adds a new "Other" batch type and a dedicated menu in
  Inventory > Operations.
