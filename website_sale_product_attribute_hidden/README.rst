=====================================
Website Sale Product Attribute Hidden
=====================================

This module applies the "Hide in product configuration" check of product
attributes to the online shop.

The product configurator and the combo configurator of the online shop do not
ask for the hidden attributes, and their extra prices are not added. It also
applies to the configurators of sales orders when the online shop is
installed.

It is installed automatically.
